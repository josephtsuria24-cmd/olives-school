About Olives Centre

Our Story

Founded in Bombolulu, Olives Rehabilitation Centre Primary School exists to provide quality, affordable CBC education to the community. We believe every child deserves a chance to learn, grow and thrive in a Godly environment.

🎯 Our Mission

To nurture holistic learners through quality CBC education, Christian values, and character development.

👁️ Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in CBC education, producing responsible, disciplined citizens.

💎 Core Values

Discipline, Excellence, Integrity, Godliness, Service, and Community.
